# Home surface

## Mode

Persuade: brand awareness first, exploration and enquiries second.

## Direction contract

Twist & Bloom is a tactile flower studio: warm cream, plum display type, rose-pink and soft yellow/pink/lavender/sage surfaces, original fuzzy 3D chenille pieces, and a warm, conversational voice. User requested professional-level execution, 3D animation, transitions, and a memorable playful UI. User confirmed the purpose is introducing the brand rather than prioritising ecommerce conversion.

## First viewport

Open masthead, generous left-aligned three-line display statement, concise introduction and explore CTA. A real interactive bouquet occupies the right half, with dimensional fibers, asymmetrical pastel backdrop, a handmade stamp, subtle motion, and three colour moods. Portrait screens stack the introduction before the bouquet. Essential desktop hero content should fit a laptop viewport.

## Visitor path

Brand impression → filterable collection of illustrated creation ideas → tactile studio story → workshop invitation and making sequence → practical FAQ → memorable closing brand statement and enquiry.

## Signature interaction

Visitors turn the fuzzy bouquet by dragging or using arrow keys, and change its colour mood. Motion pauses off screen, respects reduced motion, and has a global pause control. Static artwork remains available if WebGL cannot render.

## Honest content

The supplied facts are handmade pipe-cleaner products and workshops. Product images are original rendered ideas, explicitly labelled as illustrations. No fabricated metrics, prices, testimonials, dates, addresses, social handles, or confirmed inventory. No false enquiry submission or bookings.

## Review scope

Desktop and responsive widths 320/375/414/768 px, image loading, visual hierarchy, craft fidelity, typography, clipping, product interactions, enquiry preparation, clipboard, FAQ, reduced motion, and keyboard navigation.

## Detector findings

One warning: a spring easing on tactile hover details. Retained intentionally for the user's playful motion brief and the Hum theme's physical-interaction character. There are no bounce entrances or spring-driven form state changes.

## Build path

Code-led, original procedural Three.js artwork. No generated UI comp was used or approved.
